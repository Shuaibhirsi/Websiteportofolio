# Sources, confidence labels and safety wording

## Confidence rubric

| Marker | Tier | Counts as | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | High | Peer-reviewed article, NCBI/StatPearls, or the primary book, opened and read directly. For how a product behaves, its official docs read directly (for Tana: `outliner.tana.inc/learn` and `/help`). A prior session's direct read counts if it was recorded with the URL. | StatPearls table of Erikson's stages; Tana's "Nodes and references" page on trashed originals |
| 🟡 | Medium | University teaching material or faculty pages read directly; or several independent secondary sources that agree | A lecture deck on Erikson's maladaptations |
| 🟠 | Low | Search snippets, flashcards, blogs, AI summaries, community posts, or any page you could not open | A label found only in student slides; a Tana tip from a two-year-old blog post |

Rules:
- Never upgrade a tier because a claim "is well known".
- If a host was blocked, say so next to the claim ("not opened: network policy").
- Never invent a citation, page number, quote or URL. If a source is only known by title, cite it by title and mark it 🟠.
- Popular summaries (for example "the question each stage asks") are paraphrases. Label them as such, even when they are everywhere.
- A metaphor from a video or podcast ("development is a progress bar") belongs to its speaker. Attribute it, and never present it as the theory's own claim.

## How to label inside Tana

- Put the tier marker at the end of the line it applies to: `Ruthlessness: pushing ahead without regard for others (Boeree summary · 🟡)`.
- Give each reference card a 📚 Sources child listing every source with its marker, plus a URL field for clickable links.
- Keep a "📚 Sources and caveats" node on the dashboard that explains the markers once.

## Fallback wording (use instead of blocking)

- Transcript not obtainable: "Episode description only; transcript not read."
- Standard practice without a citation: "Standard practice in X; not cited evidence here."
- Proposed model: "A proposed model (Author, year), not settled science."
- Unverified label: "(unverified label · 🟠)".

## Safety and scope for self-reflection or mental-health systems

These systems touch painful material. Build them so they are gentle by default.

- **Scope line** (Start here node and skill report): "A reflection tool, not therapy, diagnosis or crisis support."
- **Crisis line:** verify the local numbers for the person's country before writing them. For an EU user, 112 is the emergency number. findahelpline.com lists helplines by country. Never write a number you could not verify.
- **Tentative language** everywhere: "can show up as", "a guess to test", "possible schema". Never write "you have X".
- **No diagnosis, no blame.** Avoid disorder labels and avoid blaming parents or others.
- **Experiments must be small and safe.** Never suggest confronting someone who caused harm, revisiting trauma alone, or changing medication. Point to a licensed professional for trauma or overwhelm.
- **AI privacy line:** "AI fields and voice send what you write to Tana AI."
- **Voice instructions:** warm, one field at a time, save nothing during the chat. If the person sounds overwhelmed or unsafe, pause and gently suggest professional support.
